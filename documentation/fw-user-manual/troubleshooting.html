Fixing problems

Troubleshooting

Symptoms, causes and fixes for the problems seen on the bench and in the field.

Symptoms and fixes

SymptomLikely causeWhat to do
Port missing, or error -71 in journalctl -kFlaky USB cable or hub.Replug the cable or try another one.
no STATUS replyBoard off, asleep or bad cable. Tags sleep when idle.Wake or power-cycle the tag; check the cable.
Board stops answering after a tool was opened at a wrong baudFraming errors on older firmware left the UART deaf.Power-cycle it. Tools always use 921600; never open the port at another baud.
Status shows HW=255The board is in its bootloader (no valid application).Flash it: htn_fw.py flash <image> over USB, or an ST-Link.
Board resets every minute and never startsBootloader found no valid CRC and no new image arrives within 60 s.Flash a good image (USB or ST-Link).
FOTA scan finds nothingTarget older than 3.1.1, tag asleep, or out of range.Type the id, wake the tag (htn_fw.py wake), move closer.
link lostToo many lost frames at the data rate.Move the boards closer or start at a lower data rate.
battery too lowLoader or target under 30 %.Charge it.
wrong device kindAnchor image chosen for a tag or the reverse.Pick the matching release.
verify failed after a transferThe target rebooted with another CRC.Retry; if it repeats, flash over USB.
Distance always 0, data_status 0xF0No UWB: no_UWB set, backoff running, or tag out of range.Read STATUS errors; check the tag is awake and the id is right.
No LoRa data, data_status 0x0FTag asleep, wrong id or channel, or no_LoRa set.Wake the tag; check its id; read STATUS.
Reply fields beyond the basic ones missingREPLY_EXT is off.Enable bit 0x40 of the feature mask (opcode 22).
Ranging slow with several stationsBackoff between stations.Disable scan mode (opcode 19, ctrl 0) on a bench; on a site, accept the 21 to 430 ms spread.
Tag cannot be updated or ranged after a whileIt went to sleep.Wake it by LoRa from an anchor.
Anchor 'rev D' shows no status LEDsRev D has none.Use the battery-bar LEDs 0 and 3 for FOTA.
Anchor battery reads 0 or 255No gauge wired.Expected on USB powered anchors.
st-flash cannot flash, OpenOCD failsUbuntu OpenOCD 0.12.0 has no U5A5 flash driver.Use stlink-tools 1.8.0 or newer, or STM32CubeProgrammer.
Application does not start after an ST-Link flashThe .bin went to 0x08000000 or bank 1 has no bootloader.Flash the bootloader hex first, then the application hex.

Where to look

  • STATUS reply. Version, hardware, id, error bits, LoRa and UWB counters, backoff, fault counters (anchor), link state (anchor with REPLY_EXT).
  • Fault counters. Non-zero HardFault or watchdog counts after a reset point to a crash; they persist across resets in the RTC backup registers.
  • Raw registers. READ_UWB_RAW (opcode 12) reads any DW1000 register; DW_SPI_TEST (0x4F) checks the SPI link to it.
  • Open problems. The robustness review of 2026-10-22 is in documentation/bugs/2026-10-22/buglist.md; the behaviour contract is documentation/HITAN_firmware_changes.html.
